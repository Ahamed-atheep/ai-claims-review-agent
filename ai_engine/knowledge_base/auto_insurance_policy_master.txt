APEX NATIONAL INSURANCE COMPANY - COMPREHENSIVE AUTO POLICY HANDBOOK (POLICY MASTER 2026)

PART I: COVERAGE DEFINITIONS AND LIMITS
----------------------------------------
1. Collision Coverage (Form COL-100):
   - Maximum Limit per Occurrence: $50,000.
   - Standard Deductible: $500 per claim. Deductible is waived if repairs are performed by an Apex Certified Preferred Repair Facility.
   - Covered Events: Direct physical loss to covered auto caused by accidental upset or collision with another vehicle or object.

2. Comprehensive Coverage (Form CMP-200):
   - Maximum Limit per Occurrence: $25,000.
   - Standard Deductible: $250 per claim.
   - Covered Events: Vandalism, theft, glass breakage, falling objects, animal strike, flood, or fire.

3. Bodily Injury & Medical Payments (Form MED-300):
   - Medical Payment Limit: $10,000 per injured passenger.
   - Bodily Injury Liability Limit: $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident.
   - Coverage Scope: Reasonable and necessary medical expenses incurred within 1 year of the accident date.

PART II: MANDATORY POLICY EXCLUSIONS
-------------------------------------
1. Exclusion 2.1 - Illegal Acts & Racing:
   No coverage is provided for any loss occurring while the covered auto is being operated in any competitive racing event, speed test, stunt, or while committing a felony or fleeing law enforcement.

2. Exclusion 2.2 - Driving Under the Influence (DUI/DWI):
   Physical damage coverage is excluded if the driver at the time of the accident has a Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) exceeding the statutory state limit (0.08%) or refuses chemical sobriety testing.

3. Exclusion 2.3 - Pre-Existing Damage & Wear/Tear:
   Coverage excludes pre-existing body rust, mechanical deterioration, engine wear, or un-repaired damage sustained in prior accidents before the policy inception date.

4. Exclusion 2.4 - Fraud and Intentional Misrepresentation:
   The entire policy is voided if any insured, claimant, or representative knowingly conceals material facts, exaggerates invoice costs, submits forged receipts, or makes fraudulent statements regarding a claim.

PART III: CLAIMS FILING CONDITIONS & DUTIES
--------------------------------------------
1. Reporting Deadline:
   Claims must be reported to Apex Insurance within 30 calendar days of the accident date. Failure to report within 30 days without documented incapacitation may result in claim denial.

2. Mandatory Police Report Threshold:
   A formal state or municipal police collision report is required for all claims exceeding $3,000 in physical damage, any multi-vehicle accident, or any accident resulting in bodily injury.

3. Inspection & Repair Authorization:
   No repair work exceeding $2,500 may begin without prior visual inspection and written estimate approval from an authorized Apex Insurance claims adjuster.
