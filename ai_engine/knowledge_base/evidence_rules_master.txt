SECTION 1: MANDATORY DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
1.1 POLICE REPORT MANDATE:
- A formal police accident report (Form MV-104A or regional equivalent) is STRICTLY MANDATORY for any property damage claim exceeding $3,000.00.
- Claims submitted without a police report for damage > $3,000 must be flagged for missing mandatory evidence.
- Single-vehicle collisions involving guardrails, trees, or stationary objects without a police report are high-priority investigation targets.

1.2 TOW RECEIPT TIMELINE MATCHING:
- Official tow truck operator receipts must be timestamped within 3 hours of the reported accident time.
- If tow receipt timestamp is missing, pre-dates the accident, or exceeds 6 hours post-incident without weather delay justification, flag as timeline conflict.

1.3 VEHICLE DAMAGE & VIN VERIFICATION:
- Minimum of 4 high-resolution photo evidence files required: Front, Rear, Left Side, Right Side, and VIN Plate/Door Sticker.
- Photo metadata (EXIF data) timestamp must align with reported accident date.
- Scratches or rust on structural impact points indicate pre-existing damage.

SECTION 2: GPS & METEOROLOGICAL CROSS-VERIFICATION
2.1 GPS LOCATION MATCHING:
- Claimant mobile device or telematics GPS coordinates must match reported collision location on Highway or intersection.

2.2 WEATHER VERIFICATION:
- Meteorological records for collision timestamp are verified against claimant statements (e.g., wet pavement vs clear weather records).
