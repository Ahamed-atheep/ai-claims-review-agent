===================================================================
APEX INSURANCE COMPANY - STANDARD AUTO COLLISION POLICY (POL-2026-X)
===================================================================

SECTION 1: COVERAGE LIMITS
--------------------------
1.1 Collision Coverage Limit: $50,000 per incident.
1.2 Comprehensive Coverage Limit: $25,000 per incident.
1.3 Medical Payments Coverage Limit: $10,000 per injured passenger.
1.4 Standard Deductible: $500 per collision claim unless pre-approved authorized facility is used.

SECTION 2: EXCLUSIONS & CONDITIONS
-----------------------------------
2.1 Racing & Reckless Conduct: Damages resulting from street racing, organized stunts, or driving under the influence (DUI) are strictly excluded from coverage.
2.2 Pre-existing Damage: Pre-existing bodywork damage, rust, or mechanical failures prior to the reported accident date are excluded.
2.3 Intentional Misrepresentation: Any false statement, fraudulent document, or inflated repair invoice voids the entire claim.

SECTION 3: CLAIM REPORTING DEADLINES
-------------------------------------
3.1 Incident Reporting Window: Claims must be filed within 30 calendar days of the accident date.
3.2 Police Report Requirement: Police reports are mandatory for claims exceeding $5,000 or involving multi-vehicle collisions.
3.3 Repair Authorization: All vehicle repair estimates exceeding $3,000 require prior inspection and authorization by an Apex claims adjuster.
